02
The Pain Points

The Information Chaos

💬

WhatsApp Fatigue

Important notices get buried in 20+ unrelated chats and memes.

📋

Ghost Boards

Physical notice boards are outdated and ignored by modern students.

🕵️

Scattered Answers

No central place to ask seniors for advice or find lost items.

Students waste 15+ minutes daily just searching for basic info.

03
The Solution

CampusOne: The Unified Hub

  • ✅ Verified Feed: Reliable news only.
  • ✅ Event Hub: 1-click registration.
  • ✅ Study Groups: Peer-to-peer collab.
  • ✅ Digital Lost & Found: Real-time tracking.
  • ✅ Career Portal: Internship alerts.

"Replacing 15+ disconnected groups with one intelligent ecosystem."

04
Key Innovations

Why It Works

1. Structured Categorization

Smart filters (Academics, Events, Clubs) ensure you only see what matters to you.

2. Professional Personas

Integrated "Student Identity" cards showing skills and contributions.

3. Community Q&A

Bridging the gap between juniors and seniors through "Ask Seniors" forums.

4. Action-Oriented UI

Direct "Apply" or "Register" buttons instead of static text announcements.
